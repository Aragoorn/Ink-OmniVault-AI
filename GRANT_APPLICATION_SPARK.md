### 1. What are you building and why does it matter for Ink?

Ink OmniVault AI is a modular, upgradeable smart account (ERC-4337) purpose-built for AI agents operating on Ink.

Key capabilities:
- AI Agent policies with daily spending limits and risk scores
- Time-bound + spending-limited Session Keys
- Social recovery and Passkey support
- Circuit breaker + oracle protection
- Native whitelist and helpers for Ink’s core protocols: **Tydro**, **Nado**, and **Velodrome**

This project directly supports one of Ink’s stated priorities: **AI agents infrastructure**. It gives builders a secure, controlled way to let AI agents interact with DeFi on Ink without giving them unrestricted control over funds.

Ink is positioning itself as a strong DeFi + AI-friendly chain with powerful native protocols (Tydro for lending, Nado for perps, Velodrome for swaps).
